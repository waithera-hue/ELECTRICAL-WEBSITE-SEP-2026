About Electrical Department

Building competent, innovative and responsible electrical professionals.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical and competency-based electrical training that equips learners with relevant technical skills, professional values, innovation and problem-solving abilities required in the electrical and energy industry.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in electrical technical training, producing competent, innovative and responsible professionals who contribute effectively to the development of society and industry.

Electrical Training Highlights

Practical learning experiences designed to prepare students for real electrical work environments.

Electrical Installation

Practical training in domestic and industrial electrical installations, wiring systems, circuits, switches, sockets and electrical accessories.

Electrical Wiring Practicals

Students develop practical skills in cable preparation, wiring, termination, testing and safe installation of electrical circuits.

Electrical Machines

Hands-on learning involving electrical machines, motors, transformers, generators, control systems and maintenance procedures.

Electrical Safety

Students learn electrical safety procedures, identification of hazards, use of protective equipment and safe working practices.
