Module 4 Electrical Units

Unit 1: Engineering Mathematics

Application of mathematical principles, calculations, measurements, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics and other mathematical techniques used in electrical engineering and technical workplace activities.

Unit 2: Digital Electronics

Study and application of digital electronic principles, logic gates, Boolean algebra, digital circuits, flip-flops, counters and other digital electronic systems used in electrical and electronic engineering.

Unit 3: Analogue Electronics

Application of analogue electronic principles including semiconductor devices, diodes, transistors, amplifiers, rectifiers, oscillators and analogue electronic circuits.

Unit 4: Perform Security Systems

Installation, configuration, testing and maintenance of electronic security systems including CCTV, alarms, access control systems, sensors and other security technologies.

Unit 5: Work Ethics

Application of professional values, workplace discipline, teamwork, communication, integrity, responsibility, occupational safety and ethical practices in the electrical engineering workplace.
