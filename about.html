About the Electrical Department

Building practical skills, technical knowledge and professional competence for the electrical industry.

Who We Are

The Electrical Department at Aberdare Technical College is dedicated to providing quality technical and vocational education and training in electrical engineering and related areas.

The department combines classroom instruction with practical, hands-on training to equip learners with the knowledge, skills and attitudes required to work effectively in the electrical industry.

Our training focuses on electrical installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, safety, use of electrical tools and equipment, and professional work ethics.

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in electrical technical education and training, producing competent and innovative graduates for the modern world of work.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical and competency-based electrical training through effective teaching, modern technology, practical experience and industry-oriented learning.

Our Training Areas

The Electrical Department provides practical training in a range of electrical engineering areas, including:

Electrical Installation

Installation of electrical wiring systems, accessories, circuits and electrical equipment.

Electrical Maintenance

Inspection, maintenance and repair of electrical systems and equipment.

Electrical Measurements

Use of electrical measuring instruments to test voltage, current, resistance and continuity.

Fault Finding

Identification, testing and troubleshooting of faults in electrical circuits and installations.

Electrical Safety

Application of occupational safety procedures when handling electrical tools, equipment and installations.

Practical Training

Hands-on activities that enable students to develop workplace-ready technical competencies.

Departmental Objectives

  • To provide quality competency-based electrical training.
  • To develop practical and technical skills among learners.
  • To promote electrical safety and responsible working practices.
  • To prepare learners for employment and self-employment.
  • To encourage innovation, creativity and problem-solving.
  • To promote professional ethics and discipline.
  • To prepare learners for industrial attachment and workplace activities.

Our Training Approach

The department uses a practical and competency-based approach to training. Students are encouraged to learn by doing through demonstrations, practical exercises, projects and real-world electrical installation and maintenance activities.

Our approach includes:

  • Trainer demonstrations and guided practice.
  • Individual and group practical activities.
  • Electrical installation exercises.
  • Use of appropriate electrical tools and test instruments.
  • Practical projects and problem-solving activities.
